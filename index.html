03
Sell everywhere

Built for India & the world

One store, two audiences — fully configured so an order from Jaipur and an order from New Jersey both just work. Here’s exactly how each market is handled.

🇮🇳 India-ready

Everything a modern Indian D2C store needs.

  • Payments: Razorpay / Cashfree — UPI, cards, net-banking, wallets & EMI
  • Cash on Delivery with COD rules & partial-COD option
  • Shipping: Shiprocket / Delhivery with pin-code serviceability
  • GST-compliant invoices, HSN codes & GSTIN capture
  • Pricing in ₹ with India-specific offers & free-ship thresholds
  • RTO & NDR handling to cut failed deliveries
  • WhatsApp order updates & support (optional add-on)

🌍 Globally-ready

Reach customers overseas from day one.

  • Shopify Markets: multi-currency + auto geo-location pricing
  • Payments: PayPal & international cards (Stripe where available)
  • Cross-border shipping: Shiprocket X / DHL with live rates
  • Duties & taxes handling and correct HS codes
  • Local currency display & rounding for clean prices
  • International SEO + cookie/GDPR consent for the EU
  • Translation-ready structure for future language add-ons

Payment gateways and couriers are activated against your business documents (PAN/GST, bank account). We configure and test everything; account approvals are issued by the providers.
